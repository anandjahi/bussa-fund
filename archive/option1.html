Our dollars could be doing more.

Every year, the Bajan diaspora sends over $100 million to Barbados. These gifts are a critical lifeline to the island, but they have limitations.

  • For one, instead of going towards things that build generational wealth and economic self-sufficiency almost all remittances go towards covering everyday expenses.

  • Similarly, gifts sent home reduce the Bajan diaspora's ability to save, invest, and build generational wealth ourselves.

  • Finally, individual remittances are just that: individual. Rather than pooling our resources and achieving economies of scale, each family is left to fend for themselves.

There is no way for the Bajan diaspora to pool its funds and invest them in ways that earn a return and build wealth for communities in Barbados.

The Bussa Fund is trying to change that.

Currently in a research phase, the Bussa Fund's goals are to raise investment capital from the Bajan diaspora to finance projects that contribute to a resilient, sustainable, and equitable Barbados. By supporting things like homeownership for working and middle class families, locally-owned businesses, and green infrastructure, the Bussa Fund aims to unleash the power of the diaspora to help Barbados thrive.

We are in the process of collecting non-binding commitments for investment to show that the Bajan diaspora is ready, willing, and able.
